\section{Limits and Next Steps}

The \VCL{} retains a verified fallback under finite tool gates and a composite
promotion utility. All 424 public-completed outputs pass the recorded hidden
suite, conditional on excluding 26 runs without public completion.

Claims remain system-level: evidence comprises one Vitis~2025.2/U55C campaign
per configuration, no repeats, intervals, sensitivity study, or component
ablation, and QoR covers 24 common tasks under a fixed heuristic. Moreover,
provider/run plumbing differs despite 83/86 matching source files; the provider
adapter is unsnapshotted and the DeepSeek identity lacks immutable license
evidence. Hence causal, stable-ranking, and competition-compliance claims are
unwarranted.
