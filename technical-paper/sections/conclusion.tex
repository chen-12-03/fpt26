\section{Limits and Next Steps}

The \VCL{} makes validation evidence and $Q_{\mathrm{HW}}$ the promotion
authority. The capacity-normalized resource footprint replaces
worst-single-growth with a continuous aggregate ratio. Qwen3.6-27B leads
public-gate completion at \QwenThreeSixSuccessRate. DeepSeek V4 Pro leads the
starter-anchored optimization proxy at \DeepSeekQorProxy{}, while Qwen3.5
uses the fewest tokens. These outcomes separate completion, proxy quality,
and model cost.

The proxy omits evaluator-side hidden validation and reference fallback. The
agent also depends on mature testbench inputs, a fixed toolchain
(Vitis~2025.2), and a single FPGA platform (Alveo~U55C). Matched ablations
must isolate causal mechanisms: full agent, no QoR-RAG, no
measured-failure retrieval, and no Failure Reflection; each must report
success, mean $Q_{\mathrm{HW}}$, tokens, calls, credits, and wall time.
Broader corpora and cross-platform transfer remain for future work.
