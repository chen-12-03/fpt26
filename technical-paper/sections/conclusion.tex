\section{Limits and Next Steps}

The \VCL{} makes validation evidence and \QHW{} the promotion authority.
Across \TotalModelTaskRuns{} runs, Qwen3.5 combines
\QwenThreeFiveSuccessRate{} audit-clean success with the highest score sum,
\QwenThreeFiveScoreSum. One campaign per endpoint conflates model effects,
provider failures, and sampling variation. Matched ablations must
establish the causal effects of QoR-RAG and Failure Reflection. Broader
repositories, tool versions, and devices must test transfer beyond the present
AMD corpus.
