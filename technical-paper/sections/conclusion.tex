\section{Limits and Next Steps}

The \VCL{} makes validation evidence and $Q_{\mathrm{HW}}$ the promotion
authority. The capacity-normalized resource footprint replaces worst-single-growth
with a continuous aggregate ratio, and $D$/$F$ evidence factors reward source
change and validity repair. Qwen3.6-27B, the smallest model tested, leads both
completion (\QwenThreeSixSuccessRate) and optimization quality
(\QwenThreeSixQorScore{} average on QoR tasks). Only explicit QoR optimization
tasks improve beyond the $Q_{\mathrm{HW}}{\approx}0.7576$ baseline.

The agent depends on mature testbench inputs, a fixed toolchain
(Vitis~2025.2), and a single FPGA platform (Alveo~U55C). Matched ablations
are required to isolate causal mechanisms: full agent, no QoR-RAG, no
measured-failure retrieval, and no Failure Reflection. Each variant must report
campaign identifier, success, repeated failures, mean $Q_{\mathrm{HW}}$, tokens,
calls, credits, and wall time. Broader corpora and cross-platform transfer
remain for future work.
