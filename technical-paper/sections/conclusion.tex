\section{Limits and Next Steps}

The \VCL{} makes validation evidence and \QHW{} the promotion authority. The
\TotalModelTaskRuns{} runs show that models differ sharply on structural
repair under this shared contract. One campaign per model cannot separate
model effects from provider or sampling variation, and the two-repository
suite limits external validity. Matched ablations and repeated campaigns must
establish the causal effects of QoR-RAG and Failure Reflection. Broader
repositories, tool versions, and devices will test transfer beyond the present
AMD corpus.
