High-level synthesis (HLS) agents must repair and optimize unfamiliar kernels
within limited large language model (LLM) and tool budgets. We introduce the
Verified-Candidate Loop (\VCL), which lets measured validation evidence control
candidate promotion. A bounded quality-of-results score combines effective
latency and worst-resource growth. QoR-guided retrieval-augmented generation
(QoR-RAG) reuses compatible measured evidence, while Failure Reflection turns
tool diagnostics into the next constraint. On a balanced 150-task suite built
from two fixed-commit AMD repositories, three hosted-model campaigns solve
from 88.0\% to 98.0\% of tasks. Every model solves all 25 QoR-optimization
tasks. Structural C/register-transfer-level (RTL) co-simulation repair accounts
for the largest cross-model gap.
