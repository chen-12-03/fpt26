\section{QoR Rejects False Speedups}

\QHW{} applies one objective to every task. Evaluator-side anchor selection
prefers a valid starter and falls back to a frozen reference. The agent never
receives reference source or measurements. Let $L$, $p$, and $II$ denote cycle
latency, effective period, and initiation interval; $a$ and $c$ mark the
anchor and candidate. The effective period is
$p_x=\max(p_{\mathrm{target}},p_{\mathrm{estimated},x})$. The performance and
area ratios are as follows. The weighted $II$ branch applies when the task
marks $II$ relevant and both designs report positive $II$.
\begin{align}
r_L &= \frac{p_aL_a}{p_cL_c}, &
r_P &=
\begin{cases}
r_L^{0.85}(II_a/II_c)^{0.15}, & \text{reliable }II,\\
r_L, & \text{latency only},
\end{cases}
\label{eq:performance}
\end{align}
For resource counts $A_j$ and $C_j$, define
\begin{align}
\mathcal{R}&=\{\mathrm{LUT},\mathrm{FF},\mathrm{DSP},
\mathrm{BRAM}_{18\mathrm{K}},\mathrm{URAM}\},\nonumber\\
\mathcal{R}_{+}&=\{j\in\mathcal{R}:A_j>1\lor C_j>1\},\nonumber\\
\widehat{\mathcal{R}}&=
\begin{cases}
\mathcal{R}_{+},&\mathcal{R}_{+}\ne\varnothing,\\
\mathcal{R},&\mathcal{R}_{+}=\varnothing,
\end{cases}\nonumber\\
r_A&=\left[\max_{j\in\widehat{\mathcal{R}}}
\frac{\max(C_j,1)}{\max(A_j,1)}\right]^{-1}.
\label{eq:area}
\end{align}
The score maps a weighted hardware ratio through a bounded utility:
\begin{align}
h &= r_P^{0.55}r_A^{0.45}, &
\QHW &= 1-\frac{1}{(1+h)^2}, \label{eq:qhw}\\
E &= \max\bigl(0.80,\,1-0.10u_{\mathrm{credit}}
  \nonumber\\[-2pt]
  &\hspace{4.7em}{}-0.10u_{\mathrm{time}}\bigr), \nonumber\\
S &= 100V\QHW E. \label{eq:score}
\end{align}
Here $u_{\mathrm{credit}}$ and $u_{\mathrm{time}}$ denote consumed fractions
clipped to $[0,1]$; $E$ supplies the bounded budget term in the official score
$S$. The promotion rule compares \QHW{}, after $V(c)=1$, and excludes $E$.
It rejects a cycle reduction when clock degradation or the largest
resource-growth ratio outweighs the gain.
