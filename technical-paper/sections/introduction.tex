\section{Evidence Must Control LLM Edits}

HLS engineers must interpret unfamiliar C/C++ kernels, fix functional and
streaming failures, and tune hardware under a small evaluation budget.
Simulation and synthesis reports expose correctness and performance
evidence~\cite{amd2025vitis}. An LLM can propose source changes, but its text
cannot certify an interface, register-transfer-level (RTL) execution, or a
hardware tradeoff.

LLM-assisted HLS systems automate code transformation and design-space
exploration~\cite{xiong2024hlspilot,xu2024ralad}. ChatHLS further separates
generation, directive tuning, and feedback-driven debugging
agents~\cite{li2026chathls}. General tool-using agents interleave model
reasoning and actions~\cite{yao2023react}. A metered HLS loop also needs a
candidate-promotion rule. An unchecked edit can pass compilation while failing
a hidden test, deadlocking in RTL, missing 100 MHz, or consuming more hardware
than its speedup justifies.

Our Verified-Candidate Loop (\VCL) contributes three mechanisms.
\textbf{(1)} A fail-closed interface and validation workflow preserves a
verified fallback. \textbf{(2)} A bounded hardware score \QHW{} rejects false
speedups. \textbf{(3)} QoR-guided retrieval-augmented generation (QoR-RAG) and
Failure Reflection focus later attempts on compatible evidence. We evaluate
the complete agent in \TotalModelTaskRuns{} hosted-model task runs.
