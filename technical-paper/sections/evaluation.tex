\section{Evaluation on a Frozen Task Suite}
\label{sec:evaluation}

\textbf{Protocol.} Each configuration processes the same frozen, balanced
\CorpusTaskCount{}-task suite constructed from four public HLS repositories
and released with our artifacts~\cite{fpt26repo}, with matched task budgets,
temperature, token limit, and tool timeouts. Only LLM-API infrastructure errors
are rerun; a chronological retry replaces the prior record regardless of its
outcome, leaving exactly one final record per task. Completion is the number of
completed final records divided by 150. Our separate evaluator reruns every
output that passes the public gates using finite held-out \CSim, synthesis, and
required \CoSim{} gates; these are not the organizer's final hidden benchmarks.
The score columns apply Eq.~\eqref{eq:score} to the same
\CommonQorTaskCount{} tasks.

\textbf{Results.} DeepSeek records the highest completion at
\DeepSeekSuccessCount{}/\CorpusTaskCount{} (\DeepSeekSuccessRate), followed by
Qwen3.6 at \QwenThreeSixSuccessCount{}/\CorpusTaskCount{}
(\QwenThreeSixSuccessRate) and Qwen3.5 at
\QwenThreeFiveSuccessCount{}/\CorpusTaskCount{} (\QwenThreeFiveSuccessRate).
Together, \IndependentEvaluatedCount{}/\TotalModelTaskRuns{} final
endpoint--task records pass the public gates; all
\IndependentHiddenCorrectCount{} also pass our evaluator-side held-out suite.
The remaining 26 records are retained as non-completions in the
fixed-denominator rates.

On the \CommonQorTaskCount{} tasks scoreable for every configuration, Qwen3.5
records the highest starter- and reference-anchored means. Its reference mean
is 0.37 above Qwen3.6. These QoR values use the fixed composite utility defined
in Section~III.

\textbf{Token use.} Observed all-attempt consumption, including retries, is
2.128M tokens for DeepSeek, $\geq$4.261M for Qwen3.5, and $\geq$5.398M for
Qwen3.6. Failed Qwen requests omit usage, so both Qwen totals are lower bounds.

\textbf{Reproduction and limits.} Campaigns share the controller, prompts,
gates, score, and budgets; endpoint adapters and provider reasoning differ.
Thus one campaign per configuration supports descriptive, not causal,
comparison. The public artifact repository~\cite{fpt26repo} provides code,
run records, hashes, and aggregation scripts; hosted reruns may vary.
