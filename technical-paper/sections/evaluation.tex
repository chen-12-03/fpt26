\section{Three Models Expose the Hard Cases}
\label{sec:evaluation}

\textbf{Tasks and sources.} The frozen suite contains \CorpusTaskCount{} tasks
and balances \TasksPerCategory{} tasks each across generation, compile repair,
synthesis repair, functional repair, structural \CoSim{} repair, and QoR
optimization. It derives \IntroExampleTaskCount{} kernels from AMD's Vitis HLS
introductory examples and \AccelExampleTaskCount{} from its acceleration
examples at fixed commits~\cite{amdIntroExamples,amdAccelExamples}. The builder
adds controlled faults, public tests, evaluator-only hidden tests, and frozen
references. Appendix~\ref{app:benchmark} records the complete composition and
provenance.

\textbf{Protocol.} Three campaigns call hosted models through application
programming interfaces (APIs). They use the same 150 tasks,
\CampaignCreditLimit{} credits, agent source revision, Alveo U55C target, and
Vitis 2025.2. The manifest-defined gate contract applies the Track-A runtime
requirements~\cite{fpt26tracka}. We count success only when the output passes
\CSim, \Synth, 100 MHz, capacity, and every task-applicable \CoSim{} gate. The
\RequiredCosimTaskCount{} structural tasks require \CoSim. Missing or failed
required evidence counts as failure. Reports record model identity,
hidden-test outcome, tokens, tool calls, credits, frequency, and hardware
metrics. This is strict compliance with the applicable runtime contract; the
PDF, source archive, and video remain separate submission-package checks.

\textbf{Results.} DeepSeek V4 Pro leads with
\DeepSeekSuccessCount{}/\CorpusTaskCount{} successes, the highest QoR score
(\DeepSeekQorScore), and the lowest token total
(\DeepSeekTokensM{} million). Qwen3.5 solves
\QwenThreeFiveSuccessCount{} tasks but uses \QwenThreeFiveTokensM{} million
tokens. Qwen3.6 reaches only \QwenThreeSixStructuralRate{} on structural
\CoSim{} repair, versus \DeepSeekStructuralRate{} and
\QwenThreeFiveStructuralRate{} for the other models. Structural repair
accounts for \StructuralGapTaskCount{} of its
\OverallGapTaskCount{}-success gap to Qwen3.5. Every model solves all 25 QoR
tasks, yet their mean QoR scores differ; completion rate therefore cannot
measure optimization quality by itself. Detailed category, budget, and token
results appear in Appendix~\ref{app:results}.

\takeaway{Model choice changes structural repair reliability and token cost
more than binary QoR-task completion.}
