\section{Reproduction and Boundaries}
\label{app:reproduction}

The canonical machine-readable paper dataset is
\path{technical-paper/evidence/track_a_v4_three_model_paper_data_20260913.json}.
The frozen corpus is \path{releases/track_a_150_v4_20260911}; generation and
campaign-summary scripts are \path{tools/build_track_a_v4_paper_data.py} and
\path{tools/summarize_track_a_v4_campaign.py}.

The corpus, timeout, token limit, temperature, tool timeouts, and deterministic
infrastructure-only replacement rule are matched, but retry counts differ.
The snapshots match in 83/86 tracked files, including controller, prompts,
gates, and scoring. The three differences concern provider/run-contract,
metric-recording, and runner/record-validation plumbing; the latter also
resolves provider reasoning mode. Because the execution snapshot omits the
provider adapter, neutrality cannot be established. Results therefore compare
deployed configurations, not isolated endpoint/model or component effects.
